My Health Check Scan

Scan a food barcode and see what's really in it: banned ingredients, allergens and healthier options.

What it does

Point your phone's camera at any food barcode. My Health Check Scan shows ingredients that are being removed from U.S. food or are banned in other places, explains why, and links to the official sources. It also flags your family's allergens, shows nutrition at a glance, and finds cleaner products in the same category so you can compare them side by side.

  • Barcode scanning with your phone's camera, or type the numbers
  • Flags dyes and additives being removed from U.S. food or banned elsewhere, such as Red No. 3, potassium bromate and titanium dioxide
  • Every flag explains where it's banned and why, with official sources and dates
  • Allergen alerts for milk, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, wheat, soy, fish, shellfish and sesame
  • Nutrition at a glance: sugar, salt and fat marked low, medium or high, plus Nutri-Score and processing level
  • Healthier options with a side-by-side comparison chart (Premium)
  • Your personal avoid list: artificial dyes, added sugar, seed oils, MSG and more (Premium)
  • Big-text mode for easier reading
  • Private: your settings and scans stay on your own phone, no account needed

Pricing

Scanning, banned-ingredient flags, allergens and nutrition are free. Premium adds healthier options, comparisons, your avoid list and full scan history for $2.99/month, $19.99/year or $39.99 once.
