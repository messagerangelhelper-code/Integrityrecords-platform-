MyBillPilot

Your bill-pay assistant: every bill in one place, and a safety check before every payment.

What it does

MyBillPilot keeps track of every bill you pay — utilities, subscriptions, car and home payments, insurance and more — reminds you when each one is due, and asks for your OK before you pay. Every payment page is checked by ScamShield first, so you never pay a fake bill.

  • Bills by category: subscriptions, electric, gas, water, sewer, charity, car payment, home loan and insurance
  • "Due this month" total with what's paid and what's waiting
  • Approve each payment, then pay on the company's own website
  • ScamShield checks every payment link before you pay
  • Kids' allowance tracker with chores, balances and savings goals
  • Private: bills are saved on your own device, no account needed
  • Coming soon: your own MyBillPilot card with lock and spending controls

Pricing

Free for up to 5 bills and 1 child. Premium removes the limits for $4.99/month or $49/year.
