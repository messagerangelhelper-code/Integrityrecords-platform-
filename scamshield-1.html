ScamShield

AI-powered scam detection, evidence collection, and fraud reporting assistance.

What it does

ScamShield helps people spot scams before they lose money — checking messages, links, and crypto wallet addresses against known scam patterns and databases, with extra protection built in for seniors.

  • Scam message and listing checker
  • Link checker (warning-sign analysis, website age, known-scam lists)
  • Crypto wallet address risk checker
  • Senior-friendly simple mode with trusted-contact calling
  • Insurance and charity verification tools
  • Guided FBI (IC3) fraud reporting
  • Voice input for anyone who prefers speaking over typing
