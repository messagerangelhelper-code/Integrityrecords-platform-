My Letter Helper

Ready-to-send letters for the problems everyone runs into. Fill in the blanks, then print, email or copy.

What it does

A wrong charge, a refund that never came, a debt collector who won't stop calling, a landlord who won't fix the heat. My Letter Helper gives you a clear, polite letter for each one. Type in your details and the letter fills itself in, with any blanks highlighted. Each letter tells you when to use it, any deadline that matters, how to send it, and the official sources behind it.

  • 15 letters: credit card disputes, credit report errors, debt collectors, late fees, payment plans, medical bills, refunds, cancellations, the 3-day cooling-off rule, complaints, landlord repairs, security deposits and identity theft
  • Deadlines up front, like the 60 days to dispute a credit card charge and the 30 days to ask a debt collector for proof
  • Based on guidance from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau and the Federal Trade Commission, with links to each source
  • Print it, save it as a PDF, email it, share it or copy it
  • Your name and address are saved on your phone, so the next letter takes seconds
  • My Letters: save every letter, track when you sent it, and get reminded to follow up (Premium)
  • Never asks for your Social Security number or full card number
  • One free CashCow account works here and in every CashCow app

Pricing

Every letter is free to fill in, print, email and copy. Premium adds My Letters, sent dates and follow-up reminders for $1.99/month, $14.99/year or $29.99 once.
