My Recall Check

Free safety recall checks for your car, truck or motorcycle, using official government data.

What it does

Many vehicles on the road have a safety recall that was never fixed, even though the repair is free. My Recall Check looks up your vehicle by its VIN, or by year, make and model, and shows every safety recall from NHTSA, the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration: what's wrong, the risk, and the fix. Save your vehicle and the app checks it again automatically every time you open it.

  • Look up any vehicle by VIN, or by year, make and model
  • "Do not drive" and "Park outside" warnings shown first, straight from NHTSA
  • My Garage: saved vehicles are re-checked automatically, and new recalls are flagged
  • Mark each recall as fixed and keep a record
  • Accident and damage history: free stolen and salvage check (NICB), low-cost title history (NMVTIS), and what only paid reports show
  • One free CashCow account works here and in every CashCow app

Pricing

Recall lookups, safety warnings and one vehicle in My Garage are free. Premium keeps up to 10 vehicles in My Garage, all checked automatically, for $1.99/month, $14.99/year or $29.99 once.

Try My Recall Check

Recall data from NHTSA. Recall repairs are free at the dealer. To confirm whether a specific vehicle still needs a repair, check its VIN at NHTSA.gov/recalls.
