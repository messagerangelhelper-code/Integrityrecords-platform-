My Caregiver Circle

One shared plan for the family caring for a loved one.

What it does

When a parent or loved one needs care, the details end up scattered across group texts, sticky notes and whoever happened to be at the last appointment. My Caregiver Circle puts it all in one private place the whole family shares: what's coming up, who's handling it, which medicines are on the list, and what the hospital needs to know in an emergency. Everyone sees changes within about 30 seconds.

  • Appointments with date, time, place and doctor, plus who's taking them
  • The medicine list as written on the labels, so everyone has the same copy
  • Shared to-dos with who's doing it and when it's due
  • An emergency care card: allergies, doctors, insurance and emergency contacts, with the medicine list added automatically
  • Invite family with a code or link; the circle is private to the people in it
  • One free CashCow account works here and in every CashCow app

Pricing

Free: one circle shared by 2 people, with appointments, medicines, to-dos and the care card. Premium raises it to up to 10 people per circle and up to 3 circles, and lets you print the care card, for $2.99/month, $19.99/year or $39.99 once. The organizer's Premium covers everyone in the circle.
