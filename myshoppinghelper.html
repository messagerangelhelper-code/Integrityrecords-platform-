My Shopping Helper

Type it once, then check every store you shop at.

What it does

Checking five store apps for the same item takes forever. My Shopping Helper puts your stores under one roof: type what you need once, and tap any store to see its own results and prices. Keep one shopping list for all of them, with store buttons on every item.

  • 24 stores: Amazon, Walmart, Target, Costco, Sam's Club, Kroger, H-E-B, Whole Foods, Sprouts, Aldi, Albertsons, Tom Thumb, Trader Joe's, Instacart, Wayfair, Ashley, Rooms To Go, IKEA, Dollar Tree, Dollar General, Family Dollar, Five Below, Walgreens and CVS
  • Pick "my stores" so the ones you use show first
  • One shopping list with store buttons on every item, easy to share with family
  • One free CashCow account works here and in every CashCow app

Pricing

Searching every store and one shopping list are free. Premium adds more lists, price notes that show the lowest price you found, and restock reminders, for $1.99/month, $14.99/year or $29.99 once.

Try My Shopping Helper

My Shopping Helper isn't connected to these stores. Buttons open each store's own website, where prices and stock are set by the store. CashCow may earn a commission on purchases.

Want your own version?

My Shopping Helper is built to rebrand easily for a local store group, a church or community pantry, or a deals site. I can also add your own stores.
