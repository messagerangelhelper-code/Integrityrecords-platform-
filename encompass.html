Encompass

Rideshare transportation, built around reliability and driver-rider trust.

What it does

  • Rider sign-in and ride requests, with live fare estimates
  • Driver sign-in, vehicle info, and online/offline status
  • Live map with pickup, drop-off, and driver location
  • In-app chat between rider and driver during a trip
  • In-app navigation handoff to Waze for turn-by-turn driving
  • Driver safety toolkit — optional on-device audio/video recording
  • Driver earnings tracking, with weekly and year-to-date breakdowns
